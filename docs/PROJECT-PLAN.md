Important Accounting Rules
- Employer NPS contribution will not be treated as normal cash income.
- CPF to NPS migration will be treated as an internal retirement-account transfer.
- CPF and NPS will not be double-counted in Net Worth.
- Actual CPF to NPS transfer will be recorded only after official confirmation.
- Retirement projections will be estimates based on user-defined assumptions.
